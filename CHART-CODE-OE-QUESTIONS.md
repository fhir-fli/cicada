## Chronic cardiovascular disease: does chronic arterial and aortic disease count?

CDC's immunization decision-support logic (CDSi) has a patient condition "Chronic cardiovascular disease" (CDSi observation 254). It is used in one place only: the RSV vaccine 1-dose series for adults aged 50-74 years, "Administer to persons with chronic cardiovascular disease." It is not used for pneumococcal, influenza or COVID-19 vaccine; those use a separate CDSi condition, "Chronic heart disease."

Two earlier answers disagree. One said this condition is cardiac and arterial disease, and counted atherosclerosis of the aorta and limb arteries (peripheral artery disease) and aortic dissection. The other applied the pneumococcal wording, "chronic heart disease, including congestive heart failure and cardiomyopathies," and ruled all arterial and aortic disease out.
